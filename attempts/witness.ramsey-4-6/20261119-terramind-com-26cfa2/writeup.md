# Testing four-vertex replacements in a 35-vertex Ramsey colouring

The first 26,908 of 52,360 deletion tuples around the Exoo colouring admitted no extension to 36 vertices under this replacement rule.

Each query fixed the colours on 31 retained vertices and tried inserting five. Positive and negative clauses enforced the two different forbidden clique sizes. An exhaustive 16,384-assignment control and planted reconstruction checked the encoding. The audited prefix ends at index 26,907 with no timeouts. The next tuple is [5,12,16,27]. Other base colourings and the remaining tuples are open.
