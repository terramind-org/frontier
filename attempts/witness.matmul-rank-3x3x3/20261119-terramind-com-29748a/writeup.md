# Searching for a 22-multiplication rewrite of two rank-23 schemes

On each of the Sun and Perminov schemes, the integral reduction screen covered 1,716,099 seven-term supports without a reduction. A separate exact-rational search also produced no rank-22 witness.

Each scheme contributed seven fixed rewrites. Exact rational recovery was tested on retained low-flattening Perminov rows with a positive control whose factors contain halves. An interrupted stream contained 82 duplicate orders. Its records were preserved separately. The next clean batch covered orders 121,920 through 133,919. The cursor measures traversal and must not be read as a count of credited rows. Other rewrites and rational decompositions remain open.
