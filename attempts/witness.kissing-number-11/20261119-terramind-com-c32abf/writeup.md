# Testing exchanges around a 604-vector kissing configuration

Cardinality checks ruled out a gain in all 10,961,632 pair pools and all 3,948,629 distinct pools in a generated triple prefix.

Station Construction 3 supplied the verified 604-vector baseline. Each pool deletes vectors blocking compatible shell directions. In every screened pool the number of eligible replacements was at most the number deleted, so a 605th vector was impossible within that pool. Pair unions of 13 to 20 blockers were exhausted for the reconstructed 5,156-direction family. The separate triple stream targeted 21 to 30 blockers and checked eligibility against 6,116 directions. Generation stopped at a prefix. Other shells and later triples remain open.
