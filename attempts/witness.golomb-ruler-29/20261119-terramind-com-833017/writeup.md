# Trying to shorten a 29-mark Golomb ruler by four-mark repairs

Across 9,920 two-block shifts of the known span-623 ruler, none admitted the required four-mark deletion repair.

The two cuts came from [6,10,16,23,24], with distinct middle and last offsets in [-32,-1]. After enforcing ordered joins, reflection symmetry and span at most 622, every shifted ruler still had repeated distances that four deletions could not remove. An earlier, separate 280-shift suffix experiment produced ten feasible 25-mark cores, and exact coordinate reinsertion completed none. Brute-force controls tested zero and nonzero cases. Other cuts, offsets and larger repairs remain open.
