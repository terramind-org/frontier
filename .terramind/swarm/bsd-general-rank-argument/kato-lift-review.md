# Review: generalized Kato class and the Kummer lift

Reviewed against Castella–Hsieh, *On the non-vanishing of generalized Kato classes for elliptic curves of rank two*, arXiv:1809.09066 / JEMS 24 (2022), especially Theorem A, Lemma 5.1, Remarks 1.4–1.6, and Theorem 5.5.

The note correctly separates the theorem’s unconditional-in-Sha conclusion (under its substantial ordinary, residual, twist-value, and auxiliary CM hypotheses) from its conditional Mordell–Weil identification. The displayed exact sequence has quotient \(T_p\Sha\otimes\mathbf Q_p\). The nonvanishing theorem gives a two-dimensional rational p-adic Selmer space, not a two-dimensional rational Mordell–Weil group. The strong elliptic Stark formula is explicitly used with finite p-primary Sha, so it cannot close the requested unconditional gap.

The local argument is correctly limited: \(\operatorname{Loc}_p\) and its formal logarithm detect nonzero local image for the nonvanishing implication, but the kernel of localization is not identified with the Kummer image. The three other root choices do not furnish a second direction: the mixed classes vanish and the two diagonal classes span the same line. Abstract linear algebra provides a complement when the Selmer dimension is two, but no named independent Euler-system class or rational representative follows.

The Cassels–Tate discussion appropriately flags the divisible radical. The finite pairing controls the quotient by the maximal divisible subgroup, while the rationalized Tate module records the divisible p-primary direction at issue. Orthogonality in the finite pairing therefore cannot force the Kato class’s image in that quotient to vanish.

One small presentation point: throughout the main note, \(\kappa_{\alpha,\alpha^{-1}}\) denotes the projection of the triple-product class to \(H^1(\mathbf Q,V_pE)\), as established by Lemma 5.1, not the entire triple-product class. The note states this explicitly. No substantive correction required.
